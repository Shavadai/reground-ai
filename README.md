# Reground

**An open-source AI companion for rebuilding everyday wellbeing — gently, privately, and honestly.**

> **Status: early concept.** There is no working app yet. This repository currently holds the project's scope, safety principles and design notes. Nothing here is ready for real users.

> **Reground is not a therapist, not a medical device and not an emergency service.**
> If you are in danger or thinking about ending your life, contact your local emergency number (112 in the EU) or a crisis line in your country right now.

---

## Why Reground?

Many people go through periods of burnout, chronic stress, loneliness, emotional exhaustion or low motivation. Often the hardest part is not knowing *what* would help, but finding the energy to start: getting up at a regular time, eating properly, going outside, sending one message to a friend.

Reground aims to be a calm, supportive companion for those periods. It helps people:

- **Talk things through** in a supportive, non-judgemental conversation
- **Reflect** on how they are doing and what has been helping
- **Set small, realistic goals** instead of overwhelming plans
- **Rebuild structure** in everyday routines, one step at a time
- **Reconnect with people** — friends, family, community
- **Find professional help** when that is what's needed

## What Reground is not

Reground is designed around clear limits. It will **not**:

- Act as, or claim to be, a therapist, psychologist or doctor
- Diagnose conditions or suggest medication
- Replace professional care, or discourage anyone from seeking it
- Handle emergencies — in a crisis it points people to real, human help immediately
- Try to maximise engagement or make people dependent on it

See [SAFETY.md](SAFETY.md) for the full scope, safety principles and crisis approach.

## Principles

1. **Safety before features.** Every feature is checked against the safety principles first.
2. **Privacy by design.** Collect as little as possible. Sensitive conversations are not training data and are not sold or shared.
3. **Honest about being AI.** Reground always makes clear it is an AI, and is open about what it can and cannot do.
4. **Towards people, not away from them.** Success means people need Reground *less* over time, because their routines and relationships are stronger.
5. **Small steps.** Realistic, kind, achievable — never pressure or guilt.
